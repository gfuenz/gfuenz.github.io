Career Goals

My roadmap is built on technical growth, practical experience, and meaningful impact.

Where I want to go

My long-term goal is to become a well-rounded engineer who can contribute to innovative design, solve technical problems, and work with teams to create solutions that are both effective and user-centered. I am especially interested in areas where engineering connects directly to real-world impact, such as embedded systems, product design, and interdisciplinary technological development.

I want to build a career that allows me to keep learning while also making meaningful contributions. I see engineering as a field where curiosity, persistence, and collaboration matter just as much as technical skill. My goal is to keep refining my abilities in analysis, communication, and design so I can thrive in challenging and rewarding environments.

How I plan to get there

  • Strengthen my academic foundation through coursework in circuits, programming, and engineering design.
  • Seek internships and research opportunities to gain hands-on experience with real problems and teams.
  • Continue developing communication and leadership skills so I can contribute effectively in professional settings.
  • Expand my portfolio with projects that reflect technical growth and problem-solving ability.
  • Use each experience as a step toward a future role in engineering where I can create lasting impact.
